      How it works

      1. Paste a link

        A Pinterest pin or board, a photo from Unsplash, Pexels or Flickr, a link to a picture, or any web page. You can also paste it in the Materials search box.

      2. Crop it

        Choose a shape, turn or zoom, and frame just the part you like. Nothing is saved until you say so.

      3. Work on it

        The cropped photo opens in the Design Studio as a new photo, and the studio carries on as it does with any photo.
