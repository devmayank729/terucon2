03The Firm · Corporate legacy

Building trust into every layer.

Terucon India Private Limited is a Bengaluru-based building materials and project execution company, bringing material expertise, skilled site teams and accountable coordination to ambitious spaces.

We connect the right system, product and execution team for ACP façades, commercial interiors, false ceilings, gypsum works, flooring, civil works, painting, fabrication and glazing.

Our role is to make procurement and execution easier: understand the requirement, recommend practically, coordinate responsibly and finish with care.

  • Single-window coordinationOne team across material planning, labour, execution and finishing.
  • Practical material knowledgeRecommendations grounded in performance, budget, maintenance and availability.
  • Project-first communicationClear scope, realistic next steps and responsive coordination throughout.

From bare shell to final handover.

Typical commercial floor · 18,400 sq ft · 14 weeks

  1. Week 0

    Survey & set-out

    We measure, laser-scan and set out every line before a single material reaches site.

  2. Weeks 2–6

    Services & framing

    Ducting, power, fire and data run overhead; partitions are framed and the ceiling grid goes up.

  3. Weeks 7–12

    Surfaces

    ACP, stone, timber, glass and paint go in — everything people will touch, every day.

  4. Week 14

    Handover

    Cleaned, commissioned and handed over with drawings, warranties and a team that still picks up the phone.
